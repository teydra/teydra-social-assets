Use the official Teydra icon as the exact reference: a light-blue angular triangular outline icon.

For small chest variants:

> Dark navy cotton T-shirt. On the left chest only, place the Teydra icon as a small light-blue mark, about 5 cm wide in real-world size. No text on the left chest. No words, no `Teydra Lab`, no `ChatBot`, no invented logo, no alternate logo.

For large center variants:

> Dark navy cotton T-shirt. Put a large centered chest print on the shirt: the Teydra icon beside the exact words `Teydra Lab`. The large centered print must be clearly on the front center of the T-shirt, not on the left chest. Do not include `ChatBot`, sublabels, misspellings, or alternate logos.
